Used Car Price Prediction

Basic Neural Network (MLP 64×32) vs Analytical Linear Regression (Normal Equation). Runs fully in your browser, offline, no installs.

Data note: the dataset is simulated (1,500 cars) from realistic depreciation rules plus noise. It is not real market data. Prices are in INR lakh.

Interpretation: lower MAE/RMSE and higher R² mean a better fit. Car prices fall exponentially with age and depend on interactions (e.g. diesel and premium cars lose value faster with high mileage). A purely additive linear model cannot capture these; the neural network can.
Limitation: results do not reflect real Indian used-car market prices.
